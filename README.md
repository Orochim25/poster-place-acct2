ORIVANE is a mountain of a thousand waterfalls: tiered stone terraces cascade down the mountainside, a small town stands on the steps, and rainbow mist glows below the golden sunset. Come climb the stairway of waterfalls and stay in the terrace town to the sound of falling water.

The poster is a painterly vintage travel poster in teal and sunset gold, with the name ORIVANE set in large cream serif type above the single subtitle line.

Entry for "The Poster for a Place That Isn't There" (make.fun).
